Is there a free trial?

No. If you cancel within 30 days of making your account, you get a full refund.

What are the 2 devices?

Your main PC and a remote one, like a laptop. Link them once and the laptop can use everything on the main PC.

Does my code leave my PC?

On Pro, no. Our server keeps only your account, plan, devices, and teams. Messages between your devices are encrypted end to end.
