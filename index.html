Bot Manager: AI bots that work while you do

A desktop app for Windows to create, schedule, run, and review AI bots. Each bot does one job, like reviewing code, researching a topic, or watching for secrets, with its own rules and schedule, for one project or many.

How it works

Your bots, your PC, your plan

01

Runs on your PC

Bots run with Claude Code on your own computer and your own Claude plan. Your code and your bots' work stay on your PC.

02

Safe by default

Bots are read-only unless you give them more. They never commit, push, deploy, or publish.

03

Built for teams

Sign in with Google or GitHub to work with your team. An owner invites people by email with a one-time code.

Signing in. Bot Manager uses your Google or GitHub account only to know who you are on a team: your email address and your name. It never sees your password. The privacy policy says what is kept and how to have it deleted.
