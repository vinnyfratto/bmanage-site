What it does

More than a bot runner: a hub for your Claude projects

Bots, threads, chats, files, and the people you work with, organized the way you think about your work.

Bots that do one job

A code reviewer, a topic researcher, a secrets watch, a docs check, a weekly changelog. Start from a template or describe it in plain words. Each bot is drawn as a simple flow: when it runs, where, its job, its rules, what it may do, and where results go.

Runs on its own

On a schedule, after each commit, or when you click Run now. A Windows task runs bots even with the app closed. Each run has a token limit, and all bots share a daily one.

Inbox, Runs, and Rules

What bots find lands in your Inbox: open it in your editor, fix it with Claude, or mark it done. Every run is kept step by step with its token count. House rules, like US English or no secrets in code, apply to every bot.

Every Claude thread in one place

Your Claude Code sessions, from the Claude app and the terminal, listed by project. Open one, read it, and reply: Claude continues the same session, and it shows in the Claude app too.

A better reply box

Paste pictures, mention files with @, write lists, and queue a message while Claude works. When Claude asks before a change, a popup lets you allow it, always allow it, or say no. Pick the model and effort per message.

claude.ai chats, live

Sign in to claude.ai inside Ai Project Hub and your chats and Projects show beside your code threads. Read them, reply, start a new chat in a Project.

Groups

Put projects, threads, bots, and files together by client, product, or task, drawn as one chart. Things in a group take its color everywhere, and Focus shows only that group.

Group brains

One shared page per group: what it is about, what was decided, how the pieces connect, and what is going on now. Every bot run and reply in the group starts with it, and a keeper proposes updates as work happens.

Teams

Make a team and invite people by email with a one-time code. See who is online.

How it works

Your PC, your plan, your rules

01

Runs on your PC

Bots and replies run with Claude Code on your computer, on your own Claude plan. Your code, threads, and reports stay on your PC.

02

Safe by default

Bots are read-only unless you give them more. They never commit, push, deploy, or publish, and a bot that edits works in its own copy of the project.

03

Plain words

No YAML to learn. Say what a bot should do, pick what it may use, and read what it found. Usage is shown in tokens, the way Claude Code shows it.
