Bot Manager: AI bots that work while you do

A desktop app for Windows to create, schedule, run, and review AI bots. Each bot does one job, like reviewing code, researching a topic, or watching for secrets, with its own rules and schedule, for one project or many. Bot Manager is under development and in early use within our team.

How it works

Your bots, your PC, your plan

01

Runs on your PC

Bots run with Claude Code on your own computer and your own Claude plan. Your code and your bots' work stay on your PC.

02

Safe by default

Bots are read-only unless you give them more. They never commit, push, deploy, or publish.

03

Built for teams

One account, with email, Google, or GitHub, works in the app and here. An owner invites people by email with a one-time code.

Your account. Sign up with email, Google, or GitHub. Bot Manager uses it to know who you are on a team, and to keep your plan and your PCs. The privacy policy says what is kept and how to have it deleted.
