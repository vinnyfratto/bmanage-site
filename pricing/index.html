Questions

Before you buy

Still wondering about something? Email us.

Do I need a Claude plan?

Yes. AI Project Hub runs Claude Code on your PC under your own Claude account, on Claude Pro or Max. It never uses an API key and never charges for AI. Your Claude usage counts against your Claude plan, the same as running Claude Code yourself.

How does the holiday price work?

Sign up for Pro by January 31, 2027 and your first year is $29. It renews at $39 a year.

Is there a free trial?

No. If you cancel within 30 days of making your account, you get a full refund.

What are the 2 devices?

Your main PC and a remote one, like a laptop. Link them once and the laptop can use everything on the main PC. You can remove a device any time from your account.

Does Pro leave anything out?

No. Pro has every tool: groups and brains, two devices, bots and schedules, threads and claude.ai chats, skills and connectors, and Teams. Enterprise adds a server for your company.

What does the Enterprise server do?

It hosts your company's threads, so they're available from any approved PC, not only the one where they started. Groups can cover a department, a team, or the whole company, and every thread, project, and bot in a group works from that group's notes.

Does my code leave my PC?

On Pro, no. Projects, bots, runs, reports, and threads stay on your computer. Our server keeps only your account, plan, devices, and teams; the privacy policy lists all of it. On Enterprise, threads are kept on your company's server.

What if I stop paying?

Nothing you made is deleted. Your bots stay in their files, and your groups and brains stay on your PC.
