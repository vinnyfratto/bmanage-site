Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a Claude plan?

Yes. Ai Project Hub runs Claude Code on your PC under your own Claude account, on Claude Pro or Max. It never uses an API key and never charges for AI. Your Claude usage counts against your Claude plan, as if you ran Claude Code yourself.

Is there a free trial?

No trial. Instead, if you cancel within 30 days of making your account, you get a full refund. The Free plan is free for good.

What is the founding price?

People who take Pro before launch pay $29 a year instead of $48, and keep that price for as long as they stay on Pro.

What counts as a PC?

Each Windows computer you sign in on. Pro includes 2; each extra PC is $10 a year. You can remove a PC any time from your account to make room for another.

What happens if I go back to Free?

Nothing you made is deleted. Saved bots stop running but stay in their files, groups and brains stay, and your threads and chats keep working.

Does my code leave my PC?

No. Your projects, bots, runs, reports, and threads stay on your computer. Our server keeps only your account, plan, PCs, and teams; the privacy policy lists all of it.

How does Enterprise work?

A company buys 5 seats or more ($72 a seat a year, $60 from 25 seats) and assigns each seat by email. Each seat gets everything in Pro on 2 PCs, plus making teams and inviting people. Pay by card or invoice. For 100 seats or more, talk to us.

When can I buy?

Paid plans open at launch. Until then, Ai Project Hub is under development and every feature is included with a free account.
