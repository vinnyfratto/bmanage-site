Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a Claude plan?

Yes. Ai Project Hub runs Claude Code on your PC under your own Claude account, on Claude Pro or Max. It never uses an API key and never charges for AI. Your Claude usage counts against your Claude plan, as if you ran Claude Code yourself.

What does the holiday price mean?

Pro is $29 a year if you sign up by January 31, 2027. After that, Pro is $39 a year.

Is there a free trial?

No trial. Instead, if you cancel within 30 days of making your account, you get a full refund.

What are the 2 devices?

Your main PC and a remote one, like a laptop or a PC you work on from afar. You can remove a device any time from your account to make room for another.

Are any tools held back on Pro?

No. Every plan includes every tool: threads, claude.ai chats, bots and schedules, the Inbox, Groups and group brains, skills and connectors, and Teams. Enterprise adds a server for your company.

What does the Enterprise server do?

It hosts your company's threads, so they're always available, not only on the PC where they started. Groups can span departments, teams, or the whole company, and every linked thread, project, and bot works with that group's shared knowledge, because they work together.

Does my code leave my PC?

On Pro, no. Your projects, bots, runs, reports, and threads stay on your computer; our server keeps only your account, plan, devices, and teams (the privacy policy lists all of it). On Enterprise, threads are hosted on your company's dedicated server.

What if I stop paying?

Nothing you made is deleted. Your bots stay in their files, and your groups and brains stay on your PC.
