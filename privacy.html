Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy explains what Ai Project Hub, a desktop app and website made by VC Innovations Group ("we"), keeps about you and why. Ai Project Hub is under development; we'll update this page as it grows.

Your account

You can sign up with your email and a password, or with Google or GitHub. With email, we keep your name and email; your password is stored only as a one-way hash, never readable. With Google or GitHub, they tell us your email address and your name, and we ask for nothing else: no contacts, files, mail, calendar, or repositories. We never see your Google or GitHub password.

We use your email address to match you to invitations, since an invitation works only for the email it was sent to, and we show your name and email to the people on your teams.

What we keep on our server

  • Your name, email address, and how you sign in (email, Google, or GitHub), and whether you asked for product email.
  • Your plan (Free, Pro, or Enterprise) and its status. Once paid plans exist: the price, billing dates, and the payment service's reference numbers. Card details are handled by the payment service and never stored by us.
  • The PCs you use Ai Project Hub on, so a plan can allow a set number: a name, the app version, and when each was last used.
  • The teams you make or join, your role on each, and when you joined.
  • Invitations: the email invited and when. The invitation code itself is never stored, only a one-way fingerprint of it.
  • When your Ai Project Hub last checked in, so teammates can see you're online.

This is kept in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States, encrypted at rest. You can see your own account; teammates see only your name, email, and whether you're online.

What stays on your computer

Your bots, their runs and reports, your projects and code, your Claude threads, and your Claude account all stay on your computer. Bots run with Claude Code under your own Claude account with Anthropic, which has its own terms and privacy policy. The Ai Project Hub app keeps your sign-in on your computer encrypted by Windows.

This website

When you sign in here, your browser keeps the sign-in in its local storage until you sign out. The site uses no cookies of its own and no analytics or ads.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your information

You can leave a team or delete a team you own in Ai Project Hub at any time. To have your account and everything we keep about you deleted, email us at vfratto@vcinnovationsgroup.com from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days. You can also remove Ai Project Hub's access in your Google or GitHub account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
