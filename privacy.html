Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy explains what Bot Manager, a desktop app made by VC Innovations Group ("we"), keeps about you and why.

Signing in with Google or GitHub

When you sign in, Google or GitHub tells Bot Manager your email address and your name. We ask for nothing else: no contacts, files, mail, calendar, or repositories. Bot Manager never sees your Google or GitHub password; you sign in on Google's or GitHub's own page.

We use your email address to match you to invitations, since an invitation works only for the email it was sent to, and we show your name and email to the people on your teams.

What we keep on our server

  • Your email address and name, and which sign-in you used (Google or GitHub).
  • The teams you make or join, your role on each, and when you joined.
  • Invitations: the email invited and when. The invitation code itself is never stored, only a one-way fingerprint of it.
  • When your Bot Manager last checked in, so teammates can see you're online.

This is kept in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States, encrypted at rest. Only the account it belongs to, and the teams it is part of, can see it.

What stays on your computer

Your bots, their runs and reports, your projects and code, your Claude threads, and your Claude account all stay on your computer. Bots run with Claude Code under your own Claude account with Anthropic, which has its own terms and privacy policy. Bot Manager keeps your sign-in on your computer encrypted by Windows.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Deleting your information

You can leave a team or delete a team you own in Bot Manager at any time. To have your account and everything we keep about you deleted, email us at vfratto@vcinnovationsgroup.com from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days. You can also remove Bot Manager's access in your Google or GitHub account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
